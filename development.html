Development

How ideas become digital products.

A look at the creative workflow of STARSS STUDIO and the three fields in which it develops.

Creative Workflow

Six stages, from idea to release.

These stages describe the studio’s general approach to creating digital products.

  1. 01

    Ideation

    Exploring ideas, concepts, and possibilities.

  2. 02

    Planning

    Defining the direction, requirements, and development objectives.

  3. 03

    Design

    Establishing the visual direction, structure, and user experience.

  4. 04

    Development

    Turning concepts into functional digital products.

  5. 05

    Testing

    Evaluating functionality, performance, stability, and usability.

  6. 06

    Release

    Preparing completed projects for public availability.

—Every project is different. This workflow is a general description of how the studio works, not a fixed procedure that every project is guaranteed to follow in the same way.

Development Areas

Game Development

Software Development

Android Application Development
