Our Identity

The identity of STARSS STUDIO.

The official logo, slogan, colors, and visual direction that define how STARSS STUDIO looks and feels.

01 — Official Logo

The official logo.

Supplied by the owner and shown here exactly as designed. It is never altered, redrawn, or recolored.

Download logo (PNG)

02 — Logo Concept

A geometric star in orbit.

At the center of the logo is a faceted, metallic-silver S, built from sharp geometric planes. Behind it sits a four-pointed star, and a slim orbital ring circles the symbol, catching a single point of light.

Fine concentric and crosshair lines frame the composition on a dark charcoal field, giving the mark a precise, engineered character.

03 — Brand Meaning

What the mark expresses.

The star

The idea — a fixed point of imagination from which everything begins. It echoes the name STARSS.

The S

The studio itself: sharp, geometric, and deliberate, with a metallic finish that suggests precision and craft.

The orbit

Continuous motion and exploration — ideas that keep moving, developing, and evolving over time.

04 — Official Slogan

FROM MIND TO REALITY.

The slogan describes the journey every STARSS STUDIO creation takes: it begins as an idea in the mind and is developed, through creativity and technology, into a real and functional digital product. It is the guiding principle behind the studio’s identity and its work.

07 — Visual Direction

Premium, restrained, precise.

  • Editorial and modern. Clean grids, generous whitespace, and a clear typographic hierarchy.
  • Charcoal and silver. Dark backgrounds with metallic silver accents — no gold, no neon.
  • Subtle motion. Soft orbital movement and restrained light reflections inspired by the logo.
  • Simplicity. Every element earns its place; precision matters more than the number of effects.

08 — Brand Usage Guidelines

Using the identity correctly.

Do

  • Use the official logo exactly as supplied.
  • Keep its original proportions and colors.
  • Give the logo generous clear space on dark charcoal or graphite backgrounds.
  • Use only the official charcoal-and-metallic-silver palette.
  • Use Inter or a similar clean sans-serif for text.

Do not

  • Do not stretch, squeeze, rotate, or distort the logo.
  • Do not recolor the logo or add effects to it.
  • Do not replace the symbol or redraw it as a different mark.
  • Do not use gold, neon colors, or unrelated accent colors.
  • Do not place the logo on busy or low-contrast backgrounds.
